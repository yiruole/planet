About the Artist

Ruole Yi

"I paint the space between the real and the imagined — where objects lose their certainty and become something else."

Ruole Yi is an artist working across oil painting and digital media. Her practice explores the liminal — objects and forms suspended between worlds, caught at the threshold of transformation.

Her oil paintings are marked by atmospheric depth and a distinctive material sensibility: thick impasto passages set against luminous, hazy grounds, evoking both intimacy and vast distance.

In her digital works, she extends this investigation into motion and time, building visual environments where familiar forms dissolve into abstraction.

Physicist  ·  Artist

I was once a researcher in high-energy physics.

For a long time, I tried to understand how the world works through theorems. Yet the closer I approached the fundamental level, the more I found that the world is not built on certain propositions. This became the starting point of my practice.

My practice combines painting, moving image, and digital media, navigating between particle physics, psychoanalysis, and philosophy. I am drawn to those things that exist at the boundaries of language: experiences that resist precise articulation, and presences that are always there yet can never be directly seen.

I am especially fascinated by those moments suspended between humor and fear. When the world begins to seem absurd, people tend to reach for meaning; once meaning is established, it exposes new absurdities. My work often carries this dark ironic undertone. It neither celebrates rationality nor glorifies madness, but pushes both simultaneously toward their limits. What the viewer faces is not a stable reality, but a system perpetually generating and dissolving itself.

There, order and chaos are no longer opposites.
Reality itself begins to take on the quality of a dream.
